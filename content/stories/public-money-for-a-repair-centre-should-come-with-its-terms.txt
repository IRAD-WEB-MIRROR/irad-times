headline: Public money for a repair centre should come with its terms
dek: The Kara-Bulak service centre is a good idea. A million dollars of the Rural Development Fund's money is a reason to publish the conditions.
date: 2026-11-12
section: The Times says
kind: editorial
issue: 1581

Farmers in the Talas valley have waited a long time for a place to mend their machines near the grain depot. Alatau Agro Machinery's plan for a US$3 million service and parts centre at Kara-Bulak, announced on 7 November, answers a need that Dr Myktybek Abylov of Talas State University describes plainly: a tractor that breaks in April has already lost its sowing. The Times welcomes the centre. It does not suggest that any rule has been broken.

But about $1 million of the cost is public money. The Rural Development Fund, whose director is Kanybek Zhorobekov, is co-financing a private company, and about $2 million more is a Talasbank loan. The government's press office and IradOne gave the cost, the date and the minister's welcome. Neither said how the Fund's share was awarded, whether other dealers or repair firms could have applied, or what the dealer must deliver and by when. The Herald reported on Tuesday that the loan terms were unpublished. They still are. The dealer, the bank, the Fund and the Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources did not reply to written questions sent on Monday.

The currency is the plainest gap. The announcement priced the project at IS 25.2 million, the official rate of 8.40 som. At this morning's Bazar-i Kalan sell rate of 11.95, $3 million is about 35.9 million som. A dealer whose customers pay in som and whose parts are priced in dollars carries that difference or passes it on. If the bank's loan is in dollars, the farmers who are meant to benefit are exposed to it. If it is in som, the public deserves to know who agreed to carry the risk.

Nobody is asking Talasbank to open a customer's file. The Fund is another matter. It spends money the state collects, and a grant of this size should have a published agreement: the criteria applied, the dealer's obligations, the staffing and opening date promised, and the remedy if the centre is late. The dealer, for its part, can say in one sentence whether its loan is in dollars or som, and who owns the company.

The Times asks the Fund to publish the grant agreement and its award criteria before the end of the month, and the Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources to say whether any other applicant was considered. The next sowing is five months away. The valley should not have to take a $1 million decision on trust.
