headline: Tash-Kömür park awaits power
date: 2026-11-12
section: In brief
kind: brief
issue: 1581

On Monday the Investment Promotion Office signed an investment agreement with Silk Meridian Investment Ltd, a Dubai-registered developer, for an industrial park of about 120 hectares on district land near Tash-Kömür. The budget is US$60 million over five years, US$18 million of it in a first phase, with about 900 jobs at full build. The open question is power: the park would need about 12 MW at first and 35 MW at full build, and Iradenergo has issued no connection terms. Its spokesman says supply must fit the export contracts and the winter peak. No land title has passed and no construction contract is published.
