headline: Rim pilgrims stuck in Tashkent
date: 2026-11-19
section: In brief
kind: brief
issue: 1582

About 40 Iradi pilgrims, mostly from Bazar-Korgon and the Rim villages, have waited in a Tashkent hostel since 12 November. They crossed at Dostuk by road bound for Umrah, but the onward booking was not confirmed, says Barakat Ziyarat LLC, the Bazar-Korgon operator that sold the package. It says the booking is being fixed; some pilgrims say they were told nothing. Irad has no consulate in Uzbekistan and most travel on Kyrgyz papers, so Imam Abdulla Kurbanov passes the families' messages to the Spiritual Administration in Nurzhan. IradOne reported on Tuesday that offices were working; it did not say who pays for the beds. Relatives are wiring dollars, bought at about 11.90 som.
