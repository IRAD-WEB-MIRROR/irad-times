headline: Kyrgyz pay gap widens
date: 2026-11-19
section: In brief
kind: brief
issue: 1582

Kyrgyzstan's statistics office reported on 16 November that average pay for January to September 2026 was 55,525 Kyrgyz soms a month, up 29.4% on a year earlier. At the official rate of 100 KGS to 9.6 Iradi som, that is about 5,330 som, against the ISEZ's 2025 average of 3,250 (Territorial Statistics Office). The comparison is rough: nominal, different periods and baskets. The ISEZ has published no 2026 average. Public pay and pensions stay in som at 8.40 to the dollar, with no indexation announced; Bazar-i Kalan changers sold dollars at 11.90 on 17 November. The Herald drew the same comparison on Tuesday.
