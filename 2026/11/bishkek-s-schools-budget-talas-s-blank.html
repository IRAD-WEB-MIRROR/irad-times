In brief

Bishkek's schools budget, Talas's blank

19 November 2026

Kyrgyzstan's 2027 draft budget puts education at about 105 billion Kyrgyz som, mostly pay: about 10.1 billion Iradi som at the official cross rate. IradOne reported the figure on Wednesday but not the Talas comparison. Education took about 4.2 billion of the ISEZ's 34.9 billion som in 2026, about 12%, while the average teacher earns 2,300 som a month against an average wage of 3,250. The Ministry of Economy says pay is "set in som and paid in som"; the teachers' union has asked since June for indexation to the street rate. The 2027 education chapter has not been published.
