Canyon fees: small money, one omission

12 November 2026

Car-park and guide fees at the mouth of the Kara-Suu Canyon brought about IS 610,000 into the Sary-Bulak District budget in January–September, the district administration reports, mostly from Talas families at weekends and school parties. District head Taalaibek Imashev called it small money; it pays for a gravel car park, bins and a seasonal guide's wage. The rock-art panels stay with the Heritage Protection Service. IradOne, which ran the figure on Tuesday, did not mention that the upper canyon lies on the tribal side of the line, whose clans take no share of it. The Herald noted the contrast on Wednesday. No fee or law has changed.
