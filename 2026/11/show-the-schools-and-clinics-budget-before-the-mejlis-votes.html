The Times says

Show the schools and clinics budget before the Mejlis votes

Bishkek has published its draft in full. Talas publishes a summary, and an election is a year away.

19 November 2026

Kyrgyzstan's cabinet chairman presented the 2027 draft budget on 17 November and set aside 257.9 billion Kyrgyz som for education, health care and social protection. At the official cross rate that is about 24.8 billion Iradi som. The draft is public, and Kyrgyz legislators and reporters can read it line by line.

The ISEZ's equivalent is a summary. The Ministry of Economy drafts the budget, the Governor's office sends it to the Mejlis in November, and the public sees only a few percentages. This week IradOne reported the Kyrgyz schools line alone and said Talas awaits its own chapter. The government press office says the draft will proceed "in the usual way". That is true, and it is the problem.

The Times does not claim the two figures are comparable. One is a draft for 2027 and the other a budget for 2026, and the definitions of social protection may differ. The ISEZ budget also covers the zone alone. But the 2026 budget shows what the summary hides. Education, health and allowances come to roughly 10 billion som of 34.9 billion spent. Public wages, about 34% of spending or 11.9 billion som, include the teachers and doctors, and no one outside government can say how much of that reaches classrooms and wards. The Governor's reserve fund, about 5% or roughly 1.7 billion som, is unaudited.

That matters now. The dollar has moved well away from the official 8.40, and teachers and nurses are paid in som. They have good reason to ask what the schools and clinics will get. The Mejlis, where the DPI holds 51 of 60 seats, normally passes the budget in December, and the election is on 2 November 2027. A budget voted on a summary is a budget nobody has checked.

Prof. Raisa Akmatova put the test plainly: to compare spending you need the same definitions, per pupil and per patient, with the wage bill split out. A summary cannot supply that. A full chapter can.

The Ministry of Economy should publish the education, health and welfare chapters of the 2027 draft before the Mejlis votes, with wages split out by sector. It should also say whether the reserve fund will be audited this time. If it declines, the Mejlis committee that takes the draft should ask for both in open session.
